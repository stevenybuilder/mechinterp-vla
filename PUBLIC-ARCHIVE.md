# Public archive boundary

This GitHub repository is the publication copy of a larger local research archive. It preserves the scientific story and the code needed to inspect the methods without forcing several hundred megabytes of historical raw tables into ordinary Git history.

## Included here

- the concise README, full research map, numbers audit, and five-page plain-English report;
- ten claim-first figures, their vector PDFs, and the 19 CSV files plotted in them;
- canonical findings, preregistrations, frozen configs, analysis code, intervention code, and tests;
- all 420 raw rows, manifest, summary, and runtime log from the final matched layer-6→8 experiment;
- all 36 episode rows from its exploratory closed-loop breadth screen, plus the separately labeled stopped-panel and smoke-test records;
- all 50 episode rows, manifest, summary, and runtime log from the broad-repair side-effect screen;
- a small canonical four-way simulator video set (GIF, MP4, WebM, poster, and exact outcome metadata);
- the independently derived numerical-audit JSON;
- `FULL-ARCHIVE-SHA256SUMS`, which records the complete local archive boundary;
- `PUBLIC-SHA256SUMS`, which verifies the files actually published here.

## Not duplicated in GitHub

- the four historical JSONL tables larger than 50 MB, including the 214 MB OpenVLA-OFT attention table;
- multi-GB activation intermediates and model weights;
- machine-specific runtime snapshots and older raw logs;
- application drafts, calibration applications, superseded figures, and private working notes;
- the separate COAST, safety-steering, Cosmos, and robotics-performance project.

The omitted evidence is not silently discarded. Its paths, row counts, exclusions, and cryptographic hashes are recorded in `PROVENANCE.md`, `SOURCE-MANIFEST.md`, and `FULL-ARCHIVE-SHA256SUMS`. The complete local archive remains at `/Users/stevenyang/Documents/mats-mech-interp`.
